Why the Free plan cannot be reused

  • No Always On. The app unloads after ~20 minutes idle. Plivo and LiveKit call it while the phone is ringing and allow seconds to answer — a cold start means the caller hears silence, and the daily attendance check never fires.
  • 60 CPU-minutes a day, then stopped. Reading a scanned policy PDF is minutes of CPU each; a few uploads take the portal offline for the rest of the day.
  • 1 GB RAM. An Excel register parsed in memory plus the OCR runtime does not fit alongside the Node server.
  • No custom domain or TLS binding. No attendance.talentcarriage.com.

B1 is the first tier that removes all four. Client Hub stays on F1, unchanged.

Upgrade triggers & caveats

  • B1 → B2 when an import makes the portal slow while calls are going out.
  • Worker 0.5 → 1 vCPU when reply latency passes ~3 seconds; add replicas beyond ~15 concurrent calls.
  • B1ms → General Purpose when the Burstable server spends its CPU credit — typically the third or fourth customer.
  • One restart, once. Enabling vector changes a static server parameter and needs a ~1-minute Postgres restart, scheduled out of hours. Client Hub's data and schema are untouched.
  • Per-call vendor charges are extra — Plivo, LiveKit, Sarvam and OpenAI together come to roughly ₹2.40 per 90-second call (₹2,400 at 1,000 calls a month). Not Azure, not in the figures above.
